# Project QR codes

Three transparent QR codes in PNG and SVG formats. Each links directly to its project page, resolved relative to the supplied open-day URL. See destinations.json for exact URLs and colours.

PNG: 2052 × 2052 pixels at 300 dpi, crisp square modules, 4-module transparent quiet zone. SVG: vector version for scaling in a poster. Error correction level H.

Keep the quiet zone clear. Use Laue pink and X-ray pale yellow-green on a plain dark background; use superconductor teal on a plain light background. Do not stretch or crop. Phone cameras vary in their support for light-on-dark QR codes; scan a printed proof on more than one phone before printing the final poster.

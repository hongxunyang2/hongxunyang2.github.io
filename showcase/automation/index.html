Position. Align. Acquire.

The experiment workflow

These recordings are sped up 3×; instrument motion is not shown in real time. Pause or expand a clip to inspect the controls.

01

Move around the sample.

Translate the Meca500 arm or rotate about a defined centre of rotation. Switch between fixed laboratory axes and the changing axes of the goniometer convention.

The coordinate convention stays explicit while the sample orientation changes.

02

Locate the crystal in two views.

Use the two live IC-camera views to mark the sample position and set its centre of rotation. The same workflow then moves the sample to the X-ray alignment position.

Camera feedback connects the physical sample to the robot's positioning reference.

03

Record the diffraction image.

Set the exposure, capture a Laue image and save it through the PS-camera module. The diffraction view sits alongside the positioning controls.

Keep acquisition and sample positioning in the same experiment workspace.

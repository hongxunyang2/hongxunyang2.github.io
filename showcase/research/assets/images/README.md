The walkthrough is based on the two chapter drafts and checked against arXiv:2503.04334v2 and the published PNO/SCO article (DOI:10.1038/s43246-026-01175-0). The thesis contains draft equations, unit typos, preliminary interpretations and placeholder references; do not copy those directly into visitor-facing text. In particular, do not identify a unique charge collective-mode type, equate a phonon intensity enhancement with stronger coupling, or present Hubbard-fit parameters as model-independent measurements. The model-comparison figure has different PNO and SCO energy axes, which its caption must make clear.

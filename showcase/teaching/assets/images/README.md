- `exercise-8-preview.webp`: first page of `teaching/courses/condensed-matter/exercise_sheets/sheet8.pdf`, a three-page document. Rendered with macOS PDFKit at 1400 px wide, then resized to 1000 px wide using Lanczos and encoded as WebP, quality 94, method 6. The whole page is preserved, including its existing hidden-content notice. The page links to the original PDF, which stays in the course folder.
- `student-evaluation.webp`: the first survey chart in the reflection article, from `teaching/courses/condensed-matter/assets/questions_on_xunyang.jpg`. Encoded as WebP at its original 1439 × 1022 px size, quality 94, method 6. No chart categories or labels are cropped. The chart reports five responses; the caption preserves that context.
- The hero reuses the existing `showcase/assets/images/teaching.webp`. It remains uncropped.
